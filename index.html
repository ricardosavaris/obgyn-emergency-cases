How to play

  • Take a history, examine the patient, order tests and decide management, as in a real ED encounter.
  • High-yield choices earn points; low-yield or unsafe choices cost points. Attendings, nurses and radiologists may challenge your decisions.
  • Findings accumulate in the patient chart. The simulated clock records how long each step takes.
  • At the end you receive a debrief with your path, critical errors, teaching points and references. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
